# Class 1

## 🔢 Mathematics (*Joyful Mathematics*)

### 1. Simple Word Stories (Addition / Subtraction)
* **Description:** Short, relatable narratives using numbers up to 20. LLMs excel at generating these by swapping nouns (fruits, toys, animals) and numbers.
* **Mapped Chapters:** 
  * Chapter 6: *Vegetable Farm*
  * Chapter 4: *Making Ten*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Ravi has 4 mangoes. His mother gives him 3 more mangoes. How many mangoes does Ravi have now?"*

---

### 2. Pattern Completion (Symbolic or Numerical)
* **Description:** Identifying and extending simple repeating sequences. LLMs can easily output these using text-based shapes (`△`, `○`, `□`) or numbers.
* **Mapped Chapter:** 
  * Chapter 9: *Utsav*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Look at the pattern and write what comes next: △, ○, △, ○, ___"*  
  > *or*  
  > *"2, 4, 6, __, 10"*

---

### 3. Categorization & Sorting (Text-Based)
* **Description:** Grouping items based on attributes like rolling/sliding, size, or color. LLMs can generate lists of items and ask the user to classify them.
* **Mapped Chapters:** 
  * Chapter 2: *What is Long? What is Round?*
  * Chapter 8: *Toy Fair*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Sort the following objects into 'Rolls' or 'Slides': Ball, Book, Orange, Dice."*

---

### 4. Non-Standard Measurement Scenarios
* **Description:** Questions comparing lengths or capacities using everyday units (handspans, footsteps, cups) instead of standard units (cm/kg).
* **Mapped Chapter:** 
  * Chapter 7: *Lina's Family*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"A table is 5 handspans long. A chair is 3 handspans long. Which is longer?"*

---

### 5. Sequencing Daily Activities
* **Description:** Ordering events chronologically. LLMs can generate a jumbled list of daily routines and ask for the correct 1-2-3 chronological order.
* **Mapped Chapter:** 
  * Chapter 10: *How do I Spend My Day?*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Number these activities from 1 to 3 in the order they happen:*  
  > *(A) Eating dinner*  
  > *(B) Waking up*  
  > *(C) Going to school"*

---

### 6. Missing Number / Number Line Fill-ins
* **Description:** Testing one-to-one correspondence and number sequence recognition (1 to 20).
* **Mapped Chapters:** 
  * Chapter 3: *Mango Treat*
  * Chapter 5: *How Many?*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Fill in the missing numbers: 1, 2, __, 4, 5, __, 7"*  
  > *or*  
  > *"What number comes just after 8?"*

---

### 7. Repeated Addition (Early Multiplication)
* **Description:** Framing multiplication as adding the same number multiple times using simple concrete groups.
* **Mapped Chapter:** 
  * Chapter 11: *How Many Times?*
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"There are 3 plates. Each plate has 2 laddoos. Write the repeated addition: 2 + 2 + 2 = ___"*

---

# Class 2


## 🔢 Mathematics (*Joyful Mathematics - Class 2*)

### 1. Skip Counting & Number Sequences
* **Description:** Testing the ability to count in groups (2s, 5s, 10s). LLMs can easily generate predictable numerical sequences with missing gaps.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 2 (*Counting in Groups*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Fill in the missing numbers by counting in 5s: 15, 20, ___, 30, ___, 40."*

---

### 2. Place Value Breakdown (Tens and Ones)
* **Description:** Decomposing 2-digit numbers. LLMs can accurately generate numbers up to 100 and ask the student to separate the tens and ones.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 4 (*Decorating the House*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Write the number 47 in Tens and Ones: ___ Tens and ___ Ones."*  
  > *or*  
  > *"What is the expanded form of 82? (80 + 2)"*

---

### 3. 2-Digit Addition & Subtraction Word Stories
* **Description:** Slightly more advanced word problems than Class 1, dealing with numbers up to 100. LLMs can generate scenarios involving buying toys, collecting items, or sharing.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 5 (*Fun with Numbers*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"A shopkeeper has 45 notebooks. He sells 12 notebooks. How many notebooks are left with him?"*

---

### 4. "Who Am I?" Shape Riddles
* **Description:** Instead of showing a picture of a shape, the LLM describes its properties (edges and corners) and asks the student to name it.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 1 (*Shapes Around Us*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"I am a shape with 3 straight edges and 3 corners. What shape am I?"* *(Answer: Triangle)*

---

### 5. Calendar & Time Sequencing
* **Description:** Questions testing the rote memorization and logical sequence of days and months. LLMs have perfect knowledge of the calendar and can generate infinite "before/after" questions.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 9 (*Which Season is It?*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"What day comes just after Wednesday?"*  
  > *or*  
  > *"Which month comes before October?"*

---

### 6. Simple Currency & Money Math
* **Description:** Combining standard Indian coin/note denominations to find a total sum.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 10 (*Fun at the Fair*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Riya has one Rs. 20 note and one Rs. 10 coin. How much total money does she have?"*

---

### 7. Equal Sharing (Precursor to Division)
* **Description:** Distributing a total quantity equally among a given number of groups or people.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 8 (*Grouping and Sharing*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"12 apples are shared equally among 3 baskets. How many apples are in each basket?"*

---

### 8. Non-Standard & Standard Length Comparison
* **Description:** Comparing lengths using cm or descriptive terms (taller/shorter, longer/shorter).
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 3 (*Shadow Story*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Tree A is 15 meters tall. Tree B is 12 meters tall. Which tree is shorter?"*

---

### 9. Action & Sound Patterns
* **Description:** Extending rhythmic or alphabetic patterns.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 6 (*Beats and Steps*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Complete the pattern: Clap, Snap, Stomp, Clap, Snap, ____, Clap, ____, Stomp."*

---

### 10. Capacity Estimation (Binary Choice)
* **Description:** Comparing the volume of two everyday objects. LLMs can generate pairs of objects and ask which holds more/less.
* **Mapped Chapter:** Chapter 7 (*Rani's Gift*)
* **LLM-Generatable Example:**
  > *"Circle the one that holds more water: A spoon OR A bucket?"*
